## by samit & kam

so, digital dash.

it was never really a company to us. it was more like a group chat that got out of hand.

a bunch of friends, in the middle of covid, with nothing to do and everything to prove.

this is the story of what we built, who we built it with, and why we're moving on.

<!-- STORY: where we started -->
## where we started

let's rewind.

covid hits. the world shuts down overnight.

nobody can meet in person anymore. every business that ran on handshakes and foot traffic is scrambling to get online.

we watched it happen in real time and thought... why not us?

none of us had the résumé.\
none of us had been given a real shot.

but we could build.

so we started small. websites for local businesses. simple tools. whatever people needed to keep the lights on.

![two of us walking down the street in the early days](/images/walk.jpg)

<!-- STORY: what we built -->
## what we built

and then it snowballed.

people wanted more.

we went from websites to building entire marketplaces. in industries as far apart as how you sleep and how buildings get built.

we worked with duracell.\
we worked with the empire club of canada.\
we worked with a pile of startups that just needed one more push.

we moved dead inventory in the building materials space.\
we built sleep tracking for mattresses before it was a category.\
we took a crack at same-day delivery and all the logistics behind it.

and then the weirder stuff.

quant trading platforms.\
machine learning models.\
business intelligence tools for retail.\
and a bunch more we'd need a whole other letter for.

for a while it felt like we had a hand in everything.

and somewhere in there, the thing flipped.

it started as a way to prove ourselves. it ended up proving we didn't need to.

we already had what it took. the world just needed someone to show up.

![a one year anniversary cake with blue icing](/images/cake.jpg)

<!-- STORY: the people -->
## the people

honestly though, the projects aren't the part we're proudest of.

it's the people.

digital dash grew into a business that could pay for itself. cool.

but along the way it turned into a launch pad.

the people who worked with us early on are now at dream jobs at big tech companies.

some of them are ctos at public companies.

some of them started companies of their own.

we took a shot on people when nobody else would.\
and they took a shot with us.

that's the whole thing, really.

![the digital dash hoodie](/images/hoodie.jpg) ![standing on the digital dash rug](/images/rug.jpg) ![the two of us on the couch](/images/couch.jpg)

<!-- STORY: why we're moving on -->
## why we're moving on

okay. the hard part.

something always lingered for the two of us.

we were creating a lot of value for other people. but too often we were slowed down by stakeholders who didn't get what it takes to build something meaningful, or how fast you have to move to actually change a business.

we kept thinking... what could we do without that ceiling?

what if we stopped servicing and started building something of our own?

so that's what we're doing.

it's why we started our next company. and it's why we decided to close this chapter.
